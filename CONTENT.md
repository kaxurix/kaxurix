## Scrolling and section navigation

The mobile header uses a two-column paper menu with an olive current-page indicator. It scrolls within short viewports using native scrolling and closes when focus leaves the header. Crossing the 760px breakpoint resets its open state and transfers focus when the previously focused navigation control becomes hidden. Escape returns focus to the toggle. Without JavaScript, navigation remains visible in the document and is not height-limited. Header behavior lives in `src/components/Shell.jsx`, with mobile styles in `src/responsive.css` and regression checks in `tests/navigation.spec.js`.

Scroll behavior is managed by `src/hooks/useSmoothScroll.js` using [Lenis](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis). Smoothing remains active at every viewport size. At 760px and below, wheel distance is 90% with more responsive smoothing, touch movement follows the finger at 1:1, and touch momentum uses a moderate 1.4 inertia exponent; settings update when crossing the breakpoint. Horizontal gestures, pinch zoom, and Shift+wheel bypass the smoother. Reduced motion disables it, and Life dialogs stop the underlying page scroll. `SectionNavigation.jsx` shows a small frosted previous/next control only above 760px, with the active section name and position. It tracks scrolling, uses `data-scroll-section` landmarks across pages, and disables the respective button at each end. Section jumps ease in and out over 1–1.65 seconds according to travel distance. The controls are hidden on mobile and behind open dialogs. Preserve keyboard focus, horizontal gallery gestures, and the footer clearance beneath the floating panel.
